# Customer Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within **30 days of delivery** for any product that is unused and in original packaging.
Defective products are eligible for a refund or replacement within **12 months** of purchase.

## Non-refundable items
- Software licences once activated
- Custom-configured hardware
- Items returned without original packaging after 30 days

## Process
1. The customer contacts support with the order number.
2. Support issues a return authorisation (RMA) within 1 business day.
3. Once the item arrives at the warehouse and passes inspection, the refund is issued to the original payment method.

Refunds are processed within **5-7 business days** after inspection. Partial refunds (50%) apply when a product is returned
with signs of use but is still resaleable.

## Approval limits
Support agents may approve refunds up to **$500**. Refunds above $500 require Finance Manager approval.
