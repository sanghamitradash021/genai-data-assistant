# Shipping FAQ

**How long does delivery take?**
Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the USA and 7-10 business days internationally. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days.

**How much does shipping cost?**
Standard shipping is free for orders above $100. Below that, it costs $7.99. Express shipping is $19.99.

**Can I track my order?**
Yes. A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order ships, normally within 24 hours of payment.

**What if my package arrives damaged?**
Contact support within 7 days with photos. We will ship a replacement or issue a refund under the Customer Refund Policy.

**Do you ship to PO boxes?**
Standard shipping only. Express shipping requires a street address.
