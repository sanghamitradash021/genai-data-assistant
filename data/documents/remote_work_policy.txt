ACME CORP - REMOTE WORK POLICY

Eligibility
All employees who have completed their 3-month probation may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Fully remote arrangements require written approval from a department head.

Core hours
Remote employees must be available between 10:00 and 16:00 in their local time zone.

Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor. A one-time home-office stipend of $400 is available after probation.

Security requirements
- Company VPN must be used on any public or home network when accessing internal systems.
- Devices must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes.
- Company data must never be stored on personal cloud storage.

Working abroad
Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 days per year with HR and Legal approval, for tax and compliance reasons.
