# Acme Corp Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive **20 paid vacation days** per calendar year, accrued monthly.
Up to **5 unused days** may be carried over into the next year; anything above that expires on 31 March.
Leave requests of more than 3 consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance and approved by the line manager.

## Sick leave
Employees receive **10 paid sick days** per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
- Primary caregivers: **16 weeks** of fully paid leave.
- Secondary caregivers: **6 weeks** of fully paid leave.
Leave may be taken in one block or split into two blocks within 12 months of birth or adoption.

## Public holidays
Acme observes 10 public holidays per year. The list is published each December by HR.

## Unpaid leave
Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted after 2 years of service, subject to director approval.
